# Results

## Experimental Output

- Raw Accuracy: 24.3%
- Defer Accuracy: 24.7%
- Conflict Rate: 74.7%

## Observation

The Defer Gate achieved slightly higher accuracy than the raw model output.

A high conflict rate indicates frequent disagreement between image-only and image+lab predictions.

## Conclusion

The Defer Gate approach can improve reliability by identifying conflicting predictions and reducing potentially incorrect decisions.
